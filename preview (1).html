Biography

The Story of Mr. Bill

Mr. Bill Delbrugge served as principal of The Village School for over 20 dedicated years, guiding students and staff with steady, thoughtful leadership.

Known for his commitment to student growth, innovation, and community building, he helped shape a culture where students felt supported and challenged.

His leadership extended beyond academics—supporting programs, mentoring staff, and building a school environment that balanced tradition with forward thinking.
